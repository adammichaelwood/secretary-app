Secretary SMS Terms

Effective October 15, 2026

Program: Secretary personal reminders. Secretary is a private, single-user household assistant. It texts its owner reminders about the owner's own tasks and appointments, and confirms notes the owner has texted to it.

Who receives messages: only the account owner, at the mobile number they registered. The owner opts in by texting START to the Secretary number.

Message frequency varies, typically 1–8 messages per day.

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out: reply STOP at any time to stop all messages. Help: reply HELP, or open an issue at github.com/adammichaelwood/secretary-app.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
